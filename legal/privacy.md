**What we process and why**

| Data | Purpose | Legal basis (GDPR / LGPD) | Retention |
|---|---|---|---|
| Business contact details of prospects (name, title, work email, company, country, public source) | Contacting relevant professionals about the Service (account-based, low volume) | Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR; Art. 7 X LGPD), balanced by relevance, opt-out in every message and per-country opt-in rules | 12 months from last contact, then deleted unless you become a customer |
| Customer and reader details (name, work email, company, billing contact, tax ID) | Performing the contract, invoicing, delivering issues | Contract (Art. 6(1)(b)); legal obligation for fiscal records (Art. 6(1)(c)) | Contract term + statutory retention (5 years for Brazilian fiscal records) |
| Email engagement (delivered, bounced, complained, unsubscribed) | Deliverability and honouring opt-outs | Legitimate interest | 24 months; suppression list kept indefinitely so we never contact you again |
| Website form submissions | Sending a sample issue you requested | Consent / pre-contractual steps | 12 months |

We do not use tracking pixels for individual open tracking in the newsletter. We do not sell personal data.

**Processors.** Supabase (database, EU or US region [●]), Postmark / ActiveCampaign (email delivery, US), Paddle (payments, UK/US), NFe.io (Brazilian invoicing), Hostinger (server hosting, [region]), Anthropic (AI assistance in drafting content; no personal data of readers is sent to the model beyond what is needed to draft replies). International transfers rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses or adequacy decisions where applicable.

**Your rights.** Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection (including to legitimate-interest marketing at any time) and, in Brazil, the rights in Art. 18 LGPD. Email privacy@rarefree.com; we respond within 30 days. You may complain to your supervisory authority (for Brazil, the ANPD).

**Opt-out.** Every marketing email includes a one-click opt-out. Opting out adds your address to a suppression list we check before every send.

**Cookies.** The website uses no analytics or advertising cookies. The checkout, operated by Paddle, uses cookies necessary to process payment.

**Changes.** We will post changes here and notify customers by email for material changes.
