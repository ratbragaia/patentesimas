# Subscription and Refund Policy — PatentSonar (DRAFT, under legal review)

1. **Free sample first.** Anyone may request the current issue before paying. We prefer you decide with the product in hand.
2. **Monthly plans.** Cancel any time; access continues to the end of the paid month. The first monthly charge is refundable in full if you request it within 14 days of the first charge and have received no more than two issues.
3. **Annual plans.** Refundable in full within 30 days of the first charge. After 30 days, you may cancel and receive a pro-rata refund of the unused months minus one month, or convert the balance to seats for colleagues.
4. **Enterprise contracts.** Governed by the order form.
5. **Service failure.** If we deliver fewer than 45 weekly issues in a 12-month paid period, you may request a pro-rata credit or refund for the shortfall.
6. **How.** Email billing@patentsonar.com from the billing contact address. Refunds are issued by our merchant of record (Paddle) to the original payment method within 10 business days.
7. **Consumers.** The Service is offered to businesses. If you are a consumer under applicable law (for example EU or Brazilian consumer law), your statutory withdrawal rights apply in addition to this policy.
