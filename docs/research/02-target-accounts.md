## 4. Find-public-contact strategy (compliant B2B)

Principles: legitimate-interest B2B outreach only, hand-picked, low volume, full sender identification and one-click opt-out, `do_not_contact` suppression, and per-country rules from `docs/research/04-compliance-and-payments.md`. No purchased lists, no scraping of personal data, no personal email addresses stored beyond what the recipient shares with us.

1. **Patent attorney / agent of record.** On every public patent publication, the correspondence address and attorney/agent of record are public. For in-house-prosecuted families (common at Toyota, Shin-Etsu, TDK, Proterial, BMW, Renault, ZF) the correspondent is the company's own IP department, which identifies the department, its location and often a named in-house attorney. For outside-counsel families, the firm is a channel partner and a referral path. Source: PatentsView / EPO OPS bibliographic fields, already ingested in `src/patents/`.
