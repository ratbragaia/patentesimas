Methodology and data sources

Publications are ingested weekly from the USPTO PatentsView API, the EPO Open Patent Services and Google Patents Public Data on BigQuery, matched against a published CPC and keyword scope (H01F 1/047, 1/06, 1/08, 1/10–1/113; C04B 35/26; C01B 21/06; C22C 22/00; H02K 19/10; H02K 1/246 plus 30+ terms), deduplicated by DOCDB family, and reviewed before publication. An automated check refuses to publish any issue citing a number, date or applicant that is not in the ingested record.
